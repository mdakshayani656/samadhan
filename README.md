# 🇮🇳 SAMADHAN — समाधान

## National Civic Problem Redressal & Innovation Convergence Portal

**Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Prototype**

SAMADHAN (समाधान) is an AI-powered civic problem redressal and innovation convergence platform that connects **Citizens, Government Departments, Colleges/Universities, Students, and Industries** on a unified digital platform.

The platform transforms real-world civic and societal problems reported by citizens into **structured, verified, prioritized, and actionable challenges** using Artificial Intelligence.

---

## 🌟 Vision

> **From Citizen Problems to Real-World Solutions.**

SAMADHAN aims to create a collaborative ecosystem where citizens can report problems, government authorities can verify and manage them, students can develop innovative solutions, colleges can coordinate innovation activities, and industries can provide technical expertise and implementation support.

---

# 🚨 Problem

Many real-world civic and societal problems face several challenges:

- Problems are reported through multiple disconnected channels.
- Authorities receive incomplete or unstructured information.
- Similar problems may be reported multiple times.
- High-priority problems are difficult to identify quickly.
- Citizens have limited visibility into the progress of their complaints.
- Real-world problems are not effectively connected with student innovators.
- Colleges and universities lack a centralized platform to discover real societal challenges.
- Industries often lack a structured mechanism to identify opportunities for technical collaboration.
- There is no unified ecosystem connecting citizens, government, academia, and industry.

This creates a gap between:

**Problem Identification → Verification → Innovation → Implementation → Resolution**
